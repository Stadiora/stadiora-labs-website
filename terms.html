Aria Intelligence generates decision-support information for coaches, athletes, and performance staff. Coaches, athletes, physiotherapists, and medical professionals retain full and sole responsibility for all training, health, and performance decisions. No output from Aria Intelligence overrides professional medical advice or clinical assessment.

Not a Medical Device

Aria Intelligence has not been evaluated, cleared, or approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA), the European Medicines Agency (EMA), or any other medical device regulatory authority. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, monitor, or prevent any medical condition, disease, or injury.

Injury Risk Signals

The injury risk signals generated by Aria Intelligence are based on statistical patterns in performance and wellness data. They represent an elevated risk indicator — not a clinical prediction, diagnosis, or guarantee. An athlete may sustain an injury without any prior risk signal, and an elevated risk signal does not mean an injury will occur. Risk signals must always be assessed by qualified sports medicine or physiotherapy professionals before any clinical or training decision is made.

Return-to-Play Protocol

Return-to-play outputs are structured guidance tools that track progression through a rehabilitation framework. They do not constitute medical clearance. No athlete should return to full training or competition solely on the basis of a Stadiora Labs return-to-play output. Medical clearance from a qualified healthcare professional is required before return to play following injury.

Cycle-Aware Context

Readiness outputs contextualised by menstrual cycle data are intended to support training periodisation discussions between athletes and coaches. They are not gynaecological assessments or medical advice. Any health concerns related to the menstrual cycle should be discussed with a qualified medical professional.

Emergency Situations

Stadiora Labs products are not designed for emergency use. If an athlete is experiencing a medical emergency, contact emergency services immediately. Do not rely on platform outputs in any situation where immediate medical attention may be required.

Why Now

The Window Is
Open Now.

AI maturity, behavioral shifts in athlete data adoption, and a fragmented market have created a narrow window to build the category-defining platform for athletic intelligence.

No one has built the unified layer. That is the opportunity.

The sports performance tech market is projected to reach $10B+ by 2030. The dominant platforms today serve the top 1% — Premier League clubs, Olympic programs, NFL franchises — where budgets are unlimited. The other 99% of organized sport operates without any decision intelligence. That is the gap Stadiora Labs is building for.

AI Maturity
LLMs and real-time inference now make it possible to synthesise multi-signal athletic data into a single decision — at consumer cost. This wasn't viable three years ago.
Data Adoption Shift
Athletes across all levels are tracking training, sleep, HRV, and wellness — but getting nothing actionable back. The data is there. The decision layer is missing.
Total Fragmentation
The market is dominated by siloed, single-signal tools — GPS trackers, sleep monitors, load management apps — that don't talk to each other. No platform unifies them into a decision.
Global Multi-Sport Scale
Track & field and football are entry points. The decision engine is sport-configurable. Rugby, basketball, cricket, swimming — the same infrastructure scales to any sport globally.
Problem & Solution
The Problem

Performance training is
fragmented, manual,
and unintelligent.

Coaches are managing athletes with spreadsheets, gut feel, and disconnected apps. Athletes are logging data into tools that return charts — not answers.

The result: overtraining, undertraining, preventable injuries, and missed performance peaks. The cost of a wrong decision in sport is measured in careers.

Elite clubs spend $200K–$500K/year on performance staff to solve this problem. Everyone else guesses.

The Solution

One engine.
Every signal.
One decision.

Aria Intelligence synthesises training load, recovery, sleep, HRV, wellness, and contextual signals into a single actionable output — not a score to interpret, but a decision to act on.

Full training. Modified. Recovery only. Unavailable. Coaching recommendation attached. Confidence score included. No guesswork.

The same intelligence layer powers all four products — and is available to any developer through the Aria Intelligence API.

The Platform

One Engine. Four Products.

The Aria Intelligence engine is the core infrastructure. Each product is a purpose-built application on top of it — targeting a distinct user, sport, and use case.

Aria Intelligence API
The core infrastructure layer. Six decision endpoints available to any developer building sports products — availability scoring, injury risk, sprint readiness, return-to-play, coach attention queue, and player passport. Hardware-agnostic. Sport-configurable. No rate limits.
Aria — Track & Field Athlete App
iOS and Android app for track and field athletes. Synthesises training load, recovery, readiness, and mental state into one daily session decision. Elite-level intelligence in every athlete's pocket. Currently in testing with 60+ users.
Aria XI — Football Availability Intelligence
Converts existing squad data into one daily decision per player — full training, modified, recovery, or unavailable. Coaches stop guessing. Players stop hiding pain. Software-first deployment. No wearable purchase required.
Stadiora — Coaching Intelligence Platform
Athlete management and readiness monitoring at scale for track and field coaches and academies. A full sports science operation — athlete passport, attention queue, squad readiness — without the sports science headcount.
Competitive Advantage

Why Stadiora Wins.

The moat is not a single feature. It's the combination of decision-layer infrastructure, ecosystem lock-in, and market positioning that no single-sport or single-signal competitor can replicate.

Decision Layer, Not Data Layer
Every competitor returns data. We return a decision. That's a categorically different product — and a significantly higher perceived value ceiling.
Dual-Sided Ecosystem
Athletes and coaches both live inside the platform. Athlete data improves coach decisions. Coach feedback improves athlete models. Network effects compound with every user added.
Production-Ready Infrastructure
Cloud-native, production-deployed architecture built by a team with enterprise-scale AI experience. Not a prototype. A platform that can scale to millions of athletes on existing infrastructure.
Hardware-Agnostic Design
No wearable purchase required. Works with any data source — GPS exports, manual input, third-party integrations. Zero hardware dependency eliminates the biggest adoption barrier in emerging markets.
Science Validation
Every model is validated by our performance and rehabilitation team. University research partnership. Not generic ML — sport-science-grounded methodology that earns coach trust and drives retention.
Founder-Market Fit
Built by practitioners. Cloud AI infrastructure expertise from Microsoft at scale, combined with deep sport science domain knowledge. The product exists because the founders know the problem from the inside.

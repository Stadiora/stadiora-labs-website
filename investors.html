Why the window is open

Reading an athlete every day used to take a staff of people, so only the athletes at the top of the sport ever got it. Running that judgement through software got cheap before anyone built the version an athlete can buy.

The platforms that exist were built for clubs with budgets, and what they hand back is a chart. Aria hands back a decision, and it is already in athletes' hands while the gap is still open.

Business model

Athletes subscribe, coaches and teams contract

One engine, two ways of paying for it. Athletes buy a subscription in the app. Coaches, academies and teams sign contracts for the squad.

Consumer subscriptions

Athletes pay monthly inside the app, on iOS and on Android.

Monthly subscription $7.99 and $14.99

Coach and team contracts

Aria for Coaches is the business motion. A coach, an academy or a club signs for the whole roster rather than athlete by athlete, and every athlete brought in that way keeps using the athlete app.

Value

A support staff for about $10 a month

Aria bundles the work an athlete would otherwise have to hire five people to get. That is where the pricing power sits.

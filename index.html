Who We Are
Elite Intelligence.
No Longer
Elite-Only.

The infrastructure behind a world-class training program costs hundreds of thousands of dollars per year. We are closing that gap — built by a team across Latin America and the United States, serving athletes and coaches in the Caribbean, West Africa, and emerging markets worldwide, on the belief that access to elite intelligence should not be a function of budget.

Mission

To give everyone who trains, in every sport and regardless of access or resources, the intelligence of a world-class coaching team.

Vision

A world where everyone who trains carries an elite coach in their pocket, one that knows their body.

Meet the Team
Science Over Hype

Every claim is grounded in physiology and validated by our performance and rehabilitation team. If we can't stand behind it, we don't ship it.

Build With, Not For

Athletes, coaches, and advisors are co-builders. Their insights are product inputs — not feedback collected after the fact. Aria amplifies coaches, never replaces them.

The Gap Is Our Market

Below the Premier League club and the Olympic national program sits the majority of the world's athletic talent. No one has built for them. We have.

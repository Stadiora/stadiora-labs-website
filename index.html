The engine

Signals go in. One decision comes out.

An athlete already produces more numbers in a week than anyone has time to read. Aria reads them, weighs them against the plan and the calendar, then answers the only question that matters, what to do today.

  • Training

    Sessions done, load carried and how the week is trending against the last one.
  • Sleep and recovery

    Hours slept, how that trend moved and whether the body has absorbed the work.
  • Wearables

    Heart rate, resting heart rate and variability from the watch already on your wrist.
  • Video

    Clips of your technique, read next to everything else that happened that week.
  • Nutrition

    Fuel and hydration around the work you actually did, not a generic meal plan.
What comes back

One call for the day, in plain language, with the reasoning attached. If you disagree, you can ask Aria about it and it answers.
